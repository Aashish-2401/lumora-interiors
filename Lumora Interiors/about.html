ABOUT LUMORA

Designing spaces with meaning.

We create thoughtful interiors where aesthetics, functionality and everyday life come together.

OUR STORY

More than beautiful spaces.

Lumora Interiors was founded around a simple idea: the spaces we live and work in should feel like an extension of who we are.

We combine thoughtful planning, contemporary aesthetics and attention to detail to create interiors that are both visually refined and genuinely comfortable.

Every project is approached differently, because every client, space and story is different.

OUR APPROACH

Thoughtful design, from first sketch to final detail.

We believe good design begins with listening. Understanding how a space needs to work allows us to create interiors that feel natural, personal and lasting.

From material selection and lighting to layout and finishing touches, every decision is made with purpose.
